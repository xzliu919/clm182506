I am a PhD student in Vegetable Science at Zhejiang University. My research explores the molecular mechanisms underlying ornamental and stress-responsive traits in horticultural crops — from flower pigmentation and abiotic stress tolerance in Rosa, to stress tolerance in Cucurbitaceae crops.

我是浙江大学蔬菜学博士研究生，主要研究园艺作物观赏性状与抗逆性状的分子机制——涵盖蔷薇科（月季/蔷薇）的花色与抗逆，以及葫芦科作物的抗逆机制。

Research研究方向

01

Floral Ornamental Traits花卉观赏性状

Transcriptional regulation of anthocyanin and proanthocyanidin biosynthesis in rose, e.g. the RhMYB1–RhMYB123 positive feedback loop.

月季花青素与原花青素合成的转录调控，如 RhMYB1–RhMYB123 正反馈调控环。

02

Stress Tolerance in Rosa蔷薇科抗逆

Cold, salt and drought tolerance mediated by transcription factors and ROS homeostasis (ERF54, RmZAT10, RmNHX2, RcDREB1E–RcMYB28).

由转录因子与 ROS 稳态介导的低温、盐、干旱耐受（ERF54、RmZAT10、RmNHX2、RcDREB1E–RcMYB28）。

03

Cucurbitaceae Stress Biology葫芦科抗逆

Molecular mechanisms of stress tolerance in cucurbit crops (cucumber, melon, pumpkin, watermelon).

葫芦科作物（黄瓜、甜瓜、南瓜、西瓜等）抗逆性状的分子机制。
